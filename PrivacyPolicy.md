
# Privacy Policy for DeepFocus

**Last updated:** [March 22, 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how **DeepFocus** (also referred to as **Deep Work**) handles information when you use the app.

DeepFocus is a focus and deep work timer app. It helps users start and track focus sessions, set intentions, reflect after sessions, and view simple insights based on past activity.

## 1. Summary

In the current version of DeepFocus:

* the app is designed for **offline-first use**
* the app has **no account system**
* the app has **no login**
* the app has **no cloud sync**
* session data is stored **locally on your device**
* the app does **not sell personal data**
* the app does **not include advertising SDKs in the current release**

## 2. What Information the App Handles

DeepFocus may store the following information **on your device only**:

### a. Session Data

This may include:

* session category or type (for example: reading, writing, coding, review, work, or other)
* intention or goal text you enter before a session
* session start time
* session end time
* session duration
* completion outcome or whether you completed your intention
* optional reflection or notes you enter after a session

### b. Optional Profile Information

If the app allows personalization, it may store optional profile fields such as:

* name
* email address

In the current version, this information is stored **only on your device** and is **not sent to our servers**, because this version of the app does not use a backend service for accounts or cloud storage.

## 3. What Information Is Collected or Transmitted

In the current release, DeepFocus does **not transmit your session data or profile information to our servers**, because there is **no backend server** for this app version.

This means:

* your data is primarily stored locally on your device
* we do not create an online account for you
* we do not use your data for advertising
* we do not sell your data to third parties

## 4. How Your Information Is Used

The information stored in the app is used only to provide the app’s features, such as:

* running and saving focus sessions
* showing your selected session type and intention
* allowing post-session reflection
* generating simple insights, such as time spent and session patterns
* personalizing the app experience on your device

## 5. Local Storage

DeepFocus stores data locally on the device using on-device storage, such as a local database (for example, SQLite or a similar local storage method).

Because data is stored locally:

* your information remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back it up through your device tools
* deleting the app or clearing app storage may remove your stored data

## 6. Data Sharing

In the current release, DeepFocus does **not share** your personal information or session data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

The app does **not sell** user data.

The app does **not include advertising SDKs in the current release**.

If a future version adds online services, analytics, cloud sync, or third-party integrations, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when those features are made available.

## 7. Permissions

DeepFocus is intended to use **minimal permissions**.

If the current release does not request any special Android permissions, then no special device permissions are required for the core timer and local session tracking features.

If the app uses notifications, it may request notification permission so it can:

* alert you about session progress
* notify you when a focus session ends

Only permissions actually declared in the app’s Play Console listing and app manifest should be listed there. If a permission is not used, it should not be requested.

## 8. Data Retention

Because data is stored locally on your device, retention depends mainly on how long you keep the app and its local data.

Your data will generally remain on your device until:

* you delete specific data in the app, if that option is available
* you clear the app’s storage through your device settings
* you uninstall the app

If the app later adds export, backup, or cloud features, retention details may change and this policy will be updated.

## 9. Your Choices and Controls

Depending on the app version, you may be able to:

* edit your session entries
* delete notes or reflections
* clear app data
* uninstall the app to remove locally stored data

If there is no in-app delete feature in the current version, you can generally remove locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage through your Android device settings.

## 10. Data Security

We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-conscious by storing data locally on your device in the current version.

However, no method of storage or device security is completely risk-free. You are responsible for helping protect data stored on your device, for example by using device security features such as screen lock, encryption, and secure backups where available.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

DeepFocus is **not directed to children**.

The app is intended for general productivity and focus use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through online services in the current release, because this version does not provide account creation or server-side data collection.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that raises privacy concerns, please contact us at the email below.

## 12. International Users

DeepFocus is designed with general transparency and privacy principles in mind. In the current version, because user data is stored locally on the device and not transmitted to our servers, data handling is limited to on-device processing unless future features change that behavior.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

* app improvements
* new features
* legal or operational changes

If we make material changes, we may inform users by:

* updating the Privacy Policy inside the app, if available
* updating the app’s Google Play listing
* revising the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy

You should review this Privacy Policy periodically for the latest information.
